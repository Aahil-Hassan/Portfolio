About Me

I’m Aahil Hassan Mir, a GIS & Remote Sensing Analyst based in Gilgit, Pakistan. I specialize in transforming complex geospatial data into actionable insights for research, urban planning, and high-level decision-making. My expertise is uniquely focused on the challenges of mountainous and environmentally sensitive regions like Gilgit-Baltistan.

I leverage Python-based GIS automation, Google Earth Engine, and modern web-mapping technologies to build scalable solutions. My work spans from Land-Use/Land-Cover analysis and disaster risk assessments to terrain modeling and AI-assisted geospatial tools. By automating workflows and deploying cloud-based platforms, I focus on increasing analytical accuracy while reducing manual effort.

My mission is to bridge the gap between geospatial research and modern technology. Whether I am supporting local development in Gilgit-Baltistan or designing interactive web maps for global applications, I bring a problem-solving mindset and technical precision to every project.
